# Code of Conduct

This project adopts the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).
The full text at that link applies to every project space: issues, pull requests, discussions and
any other channel used for this repository.

## In short

- Be respectful and assume good faith.
- Criticise ideas and text, not people.
- Accept that maintainers may decline a contribution that is out of scope.
- No harassment, insults, discriminatory language or publishing of someone's private information.

## Reporting

Report unacceptable behavior privately to the maintainer through the contact options on their
[GitHub profile](https://github.com/esavard). Reports are handled confidentially.

## Enforcement

The maintainer may edit or remove comments, commits, issues and pull requests that break this code,
and may temporarily or permanently block a contributor, following the enforcement guidelines of the
Contributor Covenant.
